### Beginning work during an applicable 14-day cancellation period

If the customer wants the service to begin before the applicable cooling-off period has expired, obtain the required express request and any applicable acknowledgement separately on a durable medium. Example **customer response** (adapt after legal review):

> I expressly request that JODEL begin the agreed service on [date] before my applicable 14-day cancellation period has ended. I understand that if I cancel during that period I may have to pay a proportionate amount for services properly supplied up to cancellation. If the service is fully performed after my express request, I understand I may lose the statutory right to cancel that fully performed service where the law so provides.

Do not pre-tick this request on a website or infer it from a general enquiry. Keep a copy.
